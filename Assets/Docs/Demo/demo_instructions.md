# InfinityQube - Demo Instructions

🌌 Welcome, cosmic wanderer! Step into a universe where strategy meets precision, and cubes march to your will. You are a guardian wielding the power of cosmic markers to capture ethereal cubes drifting through space.

## Essential Controls

**Movement & Navigation:**
- **WASD**: Move your cosmic wanderer through the dimensional arena

**Marker Actions:**
- **F**: Place Unit Marker (limited charges - use wisely!)
  - Markers spawn player cubes that capture enemy cubes on contact
  - Charges regenerate automatically as cubes move

**Tutorial & Messages:**
- **K**: Skip/dismiss tutorial messages and dialogue
  - Press K when messages are paused to continue

**System:**
- **ESC**: Exit game
- **F12**: Open debug/prototyping panel (for development)

## Your Mission

Capture the gray **Unit cubes** while avoiding the dark **Infinity cubes**. Each Unit cube you successfully capture brings order to the cosmic chaos. Infinity cubes are uncapturable and dangerous - they block your player cubes from reaching Unit cubes behind them.

### Cube Types

| Cube | Color | Behavior |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Unit** | Gray | Capturable - your player cubes can capture these |
| **Infinity** | Black | Uncapturable - blocks your cubes, must be avoided |

## Tutorial Experience

This demo features **Stage 0: Tutorial - Pure Fundamentals** with **3 progressive waves** designed as a guided learning experience.

### Wave Progression

**Wave 1: Basic Movement and Capture**
- Learn marker placement and basic capture mechanics
- Understand how player cubes spawn from markers
- Introduction to Infinity cube danger

**Wave 2: Multiple Infinity Cubes**
- Plan marker placement around multiple Infinity cubes
- Learn to identify safe capture paths
- Practice strategic positioning

**Wave 3: Escape Mechanics**
- Understand cube escape penalties
- Learn that 5 Unit cubes escaping = lose bottom row of grid
- Master timing and placement under pressure

### Tutorial Features

- **Guided Sequences**: Visual highlights and messages guide you through each mechanic
- **Interactive Validation**: The tutorial pauses and waits for you to place markers correctly
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Each wave builds on previous knowledge
- **Safe Learning**: Mistakes don't immediately end the stage

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Avoid Wave Cubes
- Dont get squash by the cubes. Lead with your markers

### Marker Placement
- Player is immune to cube spawned from markers
- Place Unit Markers on tiles where you want player cubes to spawn
- Player cubes move upward (toward the top of the grid) automatically
- When a player cube collides with a Unit cube, it captures it
- Player cubes are destroyed by Infinity cubes (cannot capture them)

### Escape Penalty
- If 5 Unit cubes escape (reach the bottom of the grid), you lose the bottom row
- This permanently reduces your playable grid space
- Plan carefully to avoid letting too many cubes escape!

### Marker Charges
- You have a limited number of Unit Marker charges
- Charges regenerate automatically as cubes move forward
- Use charges strategically - you can't place markers if you're out of charges

## Tips for Success

1. **Watch the Patterns**: Observe how cubes move before placing markers
2. **Plan Ahead**: Think about where cubes will be when your player cubes reach them
3. **Avoid Infinity Cubes**: They block your path - work around them strategically
4. **Use Multiple Markers**: You can place markers on multiple tiles simultaneously
5. **Manage Escapes**: Don't let 5 Unit cubes escape or you'll lose a row!
