# Cosmic Cube Capture - Demo Instructions

🌌 Welcome, cosmic wanderer! Step into a universe where strategy meets precision, and light bends to your will. You are a guardian wielding the power of cosmic markers to capture ethereal cubes drifting through space.

## Essential Controls

**Movement & Navigation:**
- **WASD**: Move your cosmic wanderer through the dimensional arena
- **Mouse**: Look around and aim your cosmic abilities

**Cosmic Marker Powers:**
- **F**: Place light markers in cube paths (limited charges - use wisely!)
- **R**: Trigger light markers to capture cubes within their ethereal glow
- **G**: Place prime markers (covers 2x2 area for strategic positioning)
- **T**: Trigger prime markers for larger area capture

**Wave Control:**
- **ENTER**: Start the next wave when you're ready to face new challenges

## Your Mission

Capture the gray cubes while avoiding the dark ones. Each gray cube you successfully capture brings order to the cosmic chaos. Dark cubes must be avoided - they disrupt the harmonic balance you're working to achieve.

## Demo Experience

This demo features the first stage tutorial with 3 progressive waves designed as a learning experience. You'll master the art of marker placement, timing, and spatial strategy as you progress through increasingly complex scenarios.

## System Requirements

- **OS**: Windows 10/11
- **Graphics**: DirectX 11 compatible
- **Storage**: 100MB available space
- **Input**: Keyboard and mouse required

## Troubleshooting

- **Performance Issues**: Try running in windowed mode or lowering screen resolution
- **Control Issues**: Ensure no other applications are capturing keyboard input
- **Graphics Issues**: Update your graphics drivers to the latest version
