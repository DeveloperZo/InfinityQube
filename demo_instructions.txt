# Infinity Cube - Demo Instructions

🌌 Welcome, cosmic wanderer! Step into a universe where strategy meets precision, and light bends to your will. You are a guardian wielding the power of cosmic markers to capture ethereal cubes drifting through space.

## Essential Controls

**Movement & Navigation:**
- **WASD**: Move your cosmic wanderer through the dimensional arena

**Marker Mode Selection:**
- **1**: Unit Marker mode
- **2**: Matrix Marker mode  
- **3**: Recursion Marker mode
- **4**: Infinity Marker mode

**Marker Actions:**
- **F**: Place marker of current mode
- **R**: Trigger cube markers (generated from collisions)

**Wave Control:**
- **ENTER**: Start the next wave when you're ready to face new challenges
- **K**: Skip dialogue and tutorial messages

**System:**
- **ESC**: Exit game
- **P**: Restart current stage
- **Tab**: Toggle UI hints
- **F12**: Open debug/prototyping panel

## Your Mission

Capture the gray cubes while avoiding the dark Infinity cubes. Each gray cube you successfully capture brings order to the cosmic chaos. Infinity cubes are uncapturable - use strategy to work around them or paint their faces for delayed effects.

## Cube Types

| Cube | Color | Behavior |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Unit** | Gray | Basic, capturable |
| **Matrix** | Blue | Creates area capture effects |
| **Recursion** | Metallic | Requires multiple hits |
| **Infinity** | Black | Uncapturable, can be painted |

## Demo Experience

This demo features the first stage tutorial with 3 progressive waves designed as a learning experience. You'll master the art of marker placement, timing, and spatial strategy as you progress through increasingly complex scenarios.

## Logs & Analytics

The game saves logs and play data to help improve the experience:
- **Log Location**: `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\DefaultCompany\InfinityQube\Logs\`
- **Statistics**: `player_statistics.json` in the same location

If you enjoyed this cosmic journey, consider sharing your logs or stats via Discord! Your play data helps refine the universe for future wanderers.

## System Requirements

- **OS**: Windows 10/11
- **Graphics**: DirectX 11 compatible
- **Storage**: 100MB available space
- **Input**: Keyboard required

## Troubleshooting

- **Performance Issues**: Try running in windowed mode or lowering screen resolution
- **Control Issues**: Ensure no other applications are capturing keyboard input
- **Graphics Issues**: Update your graphics drivers to the latest version
- **Finding Logs**: Press F12 in-game, go to System panel, click "Open Log Directory"
